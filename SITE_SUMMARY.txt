     last deploy. The label follows the value now.

     own traffic we monetize. The sentence was also false: it claimed every
     other paid call went buyer to seller, when those are buyers paying US for
     OUR tools. The replacement claim is stronger and checkable from any 402.

  4. INTERNAL STATE STRINGS OUT OF SERVED COPY. "Listening for on-chain
     payments...", "idle", and a line arguing the demo "is not a diagram".

  5. THE SELLER PATH WAS LAST. Register sat at 95% into the marketplace body,
     after the FAQ, with no entry point near the top despite the header card
     announcing itself as "BUYER PATH". Now 5% and 81%.
